# Where High School Opportunities Live

Search these in addition to `data/programs_seed.json`. Prefer the organization's own page over
any list or blog; lists are for discovery only.

## 1. Directories (discovery)
- **Pathways to Science** - pathwaystoscience.org - searchable STEM programs, filter by
  "High School". Good for research and summer programs.
- **University pre-college / outreach pages** - search `"<university> high school summer research"`,
  `"<university> pre-college program"` for universities in or near the student's state.
- **Government** - intern.nasa.gov, navalsteminterns.us, usaeop.com, afrlscholars.usra.edu,
  training.nih.gov (high school), nist.gov/ship, doe national labs ("<lab> high school internship"
  e.g. Argonne, Oak Ridge, Fermilab, Brookhaven, Los Alamos, Sandia, NREL, LLNL, PNNL),
  usajobs.gov (filter: students).
- **State** - "<state> governor's school", state science academy, state university system
  summer programs, state department of education enrichment lists.

## 2. Local (often the best odds)
Search with the student's **city/region**, never their name:
- Hospitals and medical centers: "teen volunteer", "high school research internship"
- Museums, zoos, aquariums, science centers, botanical gardens: "teen program", "youth intern"
- City youth employment programs (e.g. summer youth employment program in large cities) - paid
- Public library teen programs, community colleges (dual enrollment, summer courses)
- Local companies/startups: "<city> high school internship" - apply the legitimacy check hard
- Local university labs - these are usually reached by cold email (`/outreach`), not postings

## 3. Field-specific
- **Math:** Ross, PROMYS, Mathcamp, HCSSiM, SUMaC, MIT PRIMES, AwesomeMath, MathILy,
  BU/Stanford/Yale-affiliated math circles. Search "math summer program high school application problems".
- **CS / AI:** university CS outreach, company high school programs (verify the current year -
  these come and go), USACO camp, Girls Who Code, AI4ALL.
- **Biomed / life science:** NIH, JAX, Broad, Rockefeller, Fred Hutch, cancer centers, state
  medical schools' "high school summer research".
- **Engineering:** BWSI, DoD lab apprenticeships, university engineering summer camps.
- **Environment / field science:** fisheries/wildlife agencies, Hutton, Student Conservation
  Association (high school crews), national parks youth programs.
- **Humanities / writing / journalism:** TASS, Iowa Young Writers, Kenyon, Princeton Summer
  Journalism, local newspaper/radio youth programs.
- **Policy / business / leadership:** YYGS, Bank of America Student Leaders, Boys/Girls State,
  Senate/House page programs, local government youth councils.

## 4. Online / school-year research
For students who can't travel: PRIMES-USA, BWSI online prerequisites, MITES Semester, remote
mentoring through cold emails, and citizen-science projects (Zooniverse) that can grow into
independent projects.

## Search hygiene
- Add the **next summer's year** to queries (e.g. "2027") and reject pages showing only old cycles
  unless the program clearly runs every year - then mark the dates unverified.
- Skip: undergraduate-only REUs (most require college enrollment - check, a few accept graduating
  seniors), pages behind logins, and lead-generation listicles that just funnel to a paid service.
