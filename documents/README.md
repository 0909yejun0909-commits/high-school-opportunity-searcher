# documents/

Drop your own material here and run `/setup` → "Read my documents". Anything helps:

- `transcript.pdf` (unofficial is fine)
- an activities list or resume (any format)
- award certificates, competition results
- past essays or a school paper you're proud of (used only to learn your writing voice)
- project write-ups, links to code/portfolio

These files stay on your computer. They are git-ignored so they never get pushed anywhere.
